### About Me

I'm a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence at Utrecht University, with a
completed BSc in Computer Science from Leiden University. My interest sits with
machine learning and data science, and that is where most of my attention has
gone: Data Mining, Advanced Machine Learning, Multi-Agent Learning, Cognitive
Modelling, Computer Vision and Natural Language Processing, with Quantitative
Data Analysis and Data Intensive Systems this year.

I finished my bachelor's with a thesis on
[Algorithms for Rummikub Puzzles](https://github.com/miguelvdw/rummikub-puzzles):
a C++ solver using recursion with memoization and bounds, closed formulas for the
group value, and a random forest that predicts puzzle difficulty from features
computable before solving. I also spent a semester as a teaching assistant for
the first-year C++ programming course at Leiden. In 2026 my team qualified for
the Benelux Algorithm Programming Contest (BAPC) through the Utrecht Programming
Championship.

Alongside my studies I work as a backend developer at Consolix, where we help
companies cut down manual work by automating their processes through custom web
applications. I'm responsible for the backend: database design, APIs, business
logic and the infrastructure it runs on. Some of that work is AI-driven, which is
where my studies and my work meet.
